Moon Board Version 1 

Most of the goals and feedbacks of this board are the same as the Planet Board since they were both designed together.
I will only keep here what will be relevent after taking into account the feedback for the Planet Board.

Goals: 
- The main goal of this board is to be able to get the absolute angle of a motor shaft via a diametric ring magnet in side-shaft position.
- The second goal is to make this board modular, which can be used in various robots (ie. RC car, drone, etc.).
- The third goal is to make the board as small as possible.

Feedback:
- The board lacks mounting pins
- The board lacks LEDs to show its state (3.3V, VCC, Motor driver fault)
- Lacks more silkscreen to avoid having to look at design files
- Lacks some protections (Local PTC fuse, TVS diode)
- Add test pins since the chosen connectors can't be gripped, so only the MagAlpha here
