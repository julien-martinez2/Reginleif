Sun Board Version 1 

This board was designed after the Planet and Moon ones, and already resolves some of their issues.

Goals: 
- The main goal of this board is to be able to supervise up to 5 Planet Boards.
- The second goal is to make this board modular, which can be used in various robots (ie. RC car, drone, etc.).
- The third goal is to make the board as small as possible, but still big enough for an RPI Zero 2 W to fit on.

Feedback:
- The board uses the same mounting pins for the RPI and the mechanical structure
- The board lacks LEDs to show its state (3.3V, VCC, Planet Board load)
- Lacks more silkscreen to avoid having to look at design files
- Add test pins since the chosen connectors can't be gripped, so mainly the STM32, BNO085/86 and some connectors
- The chosen SPDT switch is too small
