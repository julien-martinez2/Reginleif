Planet Board Version 1 

Goals: 
- The main goal of this board is to be able to control a whole leg with close control (4 motors and 4 angle sensors).
- The second goal is to make this board modular, which can be used in various robots (ie. RC car, drone, etc.).
- The third goal is to make the board as small as possible.

Feedback:
- The board lacks mounting pins
- The board lacks LEDs to show its state (3.3V, VCC, Motor driver fault)
- Lacks more silkscreen to avoid having to look at design files
- Lacks some protections (Local PTC fuse, TVS diode)
- Put two of the angle sensors (currently on the moon boards) directly on the planet board
- Add test pins since the chosen connectors can't be gripped, and even more the STM32
- Too many vias near the center of the board
- Power spread on the PCB

Most of these issues come from trying to make the board smaller.
